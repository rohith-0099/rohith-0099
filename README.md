### Rohith Chandran

I'm a full-stack and AI engineer from Kerala, India. I build AI agents and the systems that keep them honest.

I care most about AI security and verification: testing what agents actually do, red-teaming them against the OWASP LLM and Agentic Top 10, and making sure code, not the model, checks the answers.

Currently I work on the Young Innovators Programme platform at K-DISC, Government of Kerala (Next.js, Node, Express, PostgreSQL). I'm also completing a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at College of Engineering Karunagappally, and I graduate in 2027.

**What I've built**

- [RedAgent](https://github.com/rohith-0099/redagent): AI agents that test other AI agents for security flaws, then find, prove and fix them. Built with Google ADK, Gemini and Arize Phoenix.
- [TRINETRA](https://github.com/rohith-0099/trinetra): turns threat reports into MITRE ATT&CK mappings, each backed by the exact sentence from the report that proves it.
- [ValleyWatch](https://github.com/rohith-0099/valleywatch): maps flood damage from free satellite data and lists the villages cut off from the nearest hospital.
- [SayItPayIt](https://github.com/rohith-0099/sayitpayit): send or request money by voice, with a fraud check before any payment moves.
- [Hindsight](https://github.com/rohith-0099/hindsight): a Slack agent that remembers team decisions and cites where they were made.
